# Roadmap

Humfiverse's technology has moved faster than its legal foundation. The phases below are listed in the order they need to be *true*, not the order they were actually built in — where the tech is ahead of what a phase legally requires, that's stated plainly rather than glossed over.

## Phase 0 — Paper pilot: the legal foundation

One song or a small catalogue, manually verified, with a real legal vehicle and a real royalty-collection account in place — proving the whole legal-to-cash-to-token pipeline works, with real (even small) royalty income, before polishing it into a product.

**Status: not started.** No legal vehicle exists yet, no lawyer has confirmed jurisdiction or classification, and no real catalogue's income has been verified end to end. This is the actual next step everything else depends on.

## Phase 1 — Minimum on-chain pilot

Smart contracts for token issuance, milestone escrow, and payouts — first on a testnet, then a single real deployment once Phase 0's legal work is done. Royalty confirmation is still manual at this stage.

**Status: the technology is built and proven, ahead of the legal work above.** The contracts are deployed, verified, and have processed real, wallet-signed purchases and milestone confirmations on Ethereum Sepolia. What's missing: this is a public testnet, not a real legal vehicle with real royalty income behind it, and there's no royalty-payout contract yet, because there's no real royalty income to pay out.

## Phase 2 — Marketplace

Multiple catalogues in one place, a way to pay with regular currency (not just crypto), built-in identity checks, and compliant resale between holders. This is also when the advisory voting feature (see [Governance](06-governance.md)) would ship — once the right safeguards are in place.

**Status: partially prototyped, not compliant or licensed yet.** A working multi-catalogue marketplace exists, along with a prototype suitability check before buying, and basic resale listings. None of it has been reviewed by a lawyer or connects to a licensed offering. There's no way to pay with regular currency — everything settles in testnet ETH.

## Phase 3 — Scale

Move royalty confirmation from one trusted party toward something more decentralized, and — only with the correct, separate license — consider real secondary-market trading, kept clearly apart from the royalty-payout system itself.

**Status: not started, and not attempted before the phases above are real.**
