Takedown procedure

Cancelling a campaign for one of the grounds in legal/08 A-1 §1, and removing its content (A-1-bis). Notice to the artist, a reply period, a written decision, then the Safe cancels on chain and this page carries out the rest. Every step is signed by a signer of the owner Safe and recorded with its outcome; nothing is ever edited or deleted from the record.
